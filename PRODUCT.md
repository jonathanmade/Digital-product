# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Enterprise decision-makers (business and IT stakeholders at mid-to-large companies) evaluating
Jonatan Marín for a Data Engineering, BI, or AI consulting engagement. They're dealing with
fragmented enterprise data (ERP/CRM/WMS systems like SAP, Salesforce, Odoo) and need governed
pipelines, embedded BI, or an AI application built on top of that data — and are deciding whether
to bring him in for a scoped, fixed-duration engagement.

## Product Purpose

Convert visits into booked strategy calls / consulting engagements. The site exists to prove
production-grade delivery capability — real architecture, real metrics — not to read as a resume
or job-search page. (Deliberate v3 repositioning: from "personal portfolio looking for work" to
"senior consultant's services page.")

## Positioning

End-to-end delivery that bridges governed Data Engineering (production-grade Bronze/Silver/Gold
pipelines) through BI (Power BI Embedded/Direct Lake) into AI applications built on top of that
governed data — one person spanning the full chain, rather than a pure BI/dashboard freelancer or
an AI prototype builder without a governed-data foundation underneath.

## Operating Context

Jonatan works solo as an independent consultant based in Barcelona, serving enterprise clients
across Europe, and can bring in a partner/subcontractor network for larger project scopes.
Services run as scoped engagements (roughly 4–12 weeks depending on service type). The primary
conversion path is a Calendly-based "Book a Strategy Call" CTA (popup widget); there is no contact
form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real technology stack: Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, Power BI Embedded/Direct Lake, Delta Lake,
  PySpark, Azure (ADF, ADLS Gen2, Event Hubs, Synapse Analytics), with source-system integration
  experience across SAP, Salesforce, Odoo, and Zinc WMS.
- The flagship case study's client identity is intentionally anonymized ("Pharmaceutical Group ·
  Multinational" / slug `pharma-medallion-fabric`) — do not reintroduce the original client name.
- No fabricated proof: testimonials, metrics, and case studies must stay honest. The Testimonials
  section is explicitly marked pending (no invented quotes); 3 of 4 Work case-study slots are
  "Coming soon" placeholders with no invented numbers.
- "Pharma / Health" as a sector callout (Hero credibility bar) is representative of past project
  experience, not an exclusive specialization — copy should not read as pharma-only.

## Product Principles

1. Prove capability with real, production-grade detail (architecture, real metrics) over generic
   claims.
2. Never fabricate proof — testimonials, case studies, and metrics stay honestly marked pending
   until real ones exist.
3. Frame every capability (BI, data engineering, AI) as built on governed, production-grade data
   foundations — the throughline differentiator across all three services.
4. Anonymize client identities where confidentiality requires it, without inventing replacement
   specifics.
5. Minimize friction from interest to booked call — one clear CTA path via Calendly, no forms or
   gimmicks.
